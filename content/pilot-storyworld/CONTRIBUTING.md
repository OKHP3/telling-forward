# Contributing to this Storyworld

This is an invite-only storyworld. A contribution is an invitation to be
considered, not an entitlement to access, continuation, canon, payment, or
reuse.

Before sharing material, a contributor must be able to answer:

- Who created it, and who else should be credited?
- Is it original, adapted, quoted, or assisted?
- What permission is being requested?
- Should it remain personal work, become an open path, be proposed for canon,
  or be a published alternate path?
- Are there restrictions, attribution requirements, or removal requests?

Do not submit unpublished fiction, personal material, or material owned by
someone else unless you have permission to share it with this storyworld.
Repository visibility is not reuse permission.

The product uses these plain-language outcomes:

- **Personal work** — private work that is not open for continuation.
- **Open path** — a defined invitation for others to continue.
- **Proposed canon** — material a steward may review for the canon.
- **Published alternate path** — a permitted continuity that does not replace
  the canon.

The review status is separate: Draft, Submitted, Under review, Returned with
notes, Accepted into canon, or Published as an alternate path. A steward may
restrict, withdraw, or archive material according to the world policy.

GitHub forms and checks are backstage implementation. Contributors should use
the storyworld terms above and should not need to understand repositories,
branches, pull requests, or merge operations.
