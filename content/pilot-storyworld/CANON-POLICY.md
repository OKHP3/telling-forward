# Canon Policy

## Steward decision

The world steward is accountable for the canon policy, permissions,
moderation, attribution, and any commercial-rights decision. The application
steward role authorizes product actions; repository protections are defense in
depth and never replace that role.

No automation, label, check, or workflow may silently:

- accept a contribution into canon;
- publish an alternate path;
- decide that a contributor granted reuse rights;
- remove a restriction or moderation outcome; or
- treat an unverified metadata change as authorisation.

## Review outcomes

Every story submission has one explainable status:

`draft → submitted → under-review → returned-with-notes → under-review`

The review ends in exactly one of:

- `accepted-into-canon`
- `published-alternate`
- a steward-controlled terminal restriction, withdrawal, or archive outcome.

The application is the authority for these product states. GitHub history
preserves the durable creative and review evidence.

## Private pilot boundary

This kit is for synthetic or demonstrably owned content in an invite-only
pilot. Do not open public contribution, untrusted uploads, rights-sensitive
publication, derivatives, monetization, or automatic publication without an
owner-approved policy and safety review.
