# Mission

We dismantle the barriers to storytelling so more human experiences can be imagined, shaped, shared, and preserved across generations.

We believe everyone carries stories worth hearing, even when writing, editing, technology, time, or self-doubt makes authorship feel inaccessible. Telling Forward helps people begin with conversation, memory, character, experience, or possibility rather than with a blank page.

Voice-first tools and carefully guided agents reduce the mechanical burden of transcription, organization, revision, and continuity while keeping creative intent and human judgment at the center.

Contributors can develop stories individually, collaborate within shared worlds, explore alternate paths, and decide what becomes part of a lasting canon. We honor contribution through clear attribution, consent, stewardship, and transparent creative governance.

By combining accessible storytelling with durable collaboration, we create a place where ideas can take shape, communities can build together, and stories can remain available to people who have not yet been born.

## Working principles

1. **Start with the person, not the prose.** A contribution may begin as speech, memory, outline, scene intent, or a question.
2. **Assist without taking authorship.** Agents may transform, organize, and flag; contributors approve what represents their intent.
3. **Make the hidden machinery humane.** Version control, review, and automation should support the story without requiring contributors to learn developer terminology.
4. **Keep provenance visible.** Every accepted contribution should retain its source, contributor credit, and review history.
5. **Protect unfinished work.** Public visibility does not automatically mean permission to reuse, adapt, or publish.
